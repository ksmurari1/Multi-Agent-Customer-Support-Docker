Nimbus Cloud - Frequently Asked Questions

Q: What are the support hours?
A: Live chat support is available Monday to Friday, 9:00 to 18:00 IST. Email support at help@nimbus.example is answered within 1 business day.

Q: How much storage does the Starter plan include?
A: The Starter plan includes 100 GB of storage per user. The Business plan includes 1 TB per user.

Q: Can I use a {custom} domain for my workspace?
A: Custom domains are available on the Business plan only. Add a CNAME record pointing to workspaces.nimbus.example and verify it in Settings > Domains.
